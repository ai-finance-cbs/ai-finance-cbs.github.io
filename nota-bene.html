For employers: what kind of people should I hire for jobs that use AI?

Look for people who have built something with AI and can show it: a hackathon project, a side project, or a tool they made at work. Ask them to walk you through it, including where it broke and how they checked the output. That tells you more than a course certificate does.

What are good resources to stay on top of AI?

  • Techmeme: the day's tech news on one page.
  • Twitter/X: most of the new work and the discussion around it shows up here first. Some accounts to start with:
    • Finance and economics: Tyler Cowen, Noah Smith, Joe Weisenthal, Alex Imas, Luis Garicano, Erik Brynjolfsson
    • Tech and AI: Simon Willison, Andrej Karpathy, Ethan Mollick, Ben Thompson, Dwarkesh Patel, Dylan Patel
    • AI labs (new releases): OpenAI, Anthropic, Google DeepMind, Meta AI
  • Newsletters: Simon Willison's blog for hands-on tools, One Useful Thing (Ethan Mollick) for how people use AI at work, and Stratechery (Ben Thompson) for the business side.

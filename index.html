Who should take this course

The course is for students who want a career in the financial sector and expect AI to be part of the job. The final project develops an idea for the finance role you are heading toward.

The course does not teach one AI product, how models are built, or how firms deploy AI across an organization. It is not a programming course. You will read and run code that AI writes for you.

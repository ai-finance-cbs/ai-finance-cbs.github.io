One three-hour session each week: a mix of lecture, class discussion, and in-class exercises. Dates and location TBA.

Course goals

  1. To develop working fluency with AI tools in the context of finance. We will predominantly focus on producing financial analysis with AI in the formats the financial sector actually uses and requires.
  2. To establish what AI can and cannot do reliably in financial work. We will study where its answers come from, where it fails in ways particular to financial data, and how to test its output rather than take it on faith.
  3. To develop the judgment that AI-assisted work still requires. At least in the current scheme, the burden (and the liability) of the decision still falls on humans. We will practice deciding when AI-assisted analysis supports action and defending that decision under your own name.

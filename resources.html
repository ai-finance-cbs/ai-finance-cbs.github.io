Class notes. PDFs of lecture notes and other reading materials are posted on {% include canvas.html %}. You can download, view, and mark them up on your tablet or other devices.

Readings. There is no textbook for this course. The field moves too quickly for one to be useful, and part of the course is learning to read it in real time.

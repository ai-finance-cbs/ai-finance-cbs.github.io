Required Readings

Each week has a short list of required readings in the Library: articles, essays, and the occasional podcast episode. Please complete them before class. Each class begins with a short quiz on that week's required readings, done on paper without devices. There are five quizzes, worth 3 points each. The recommended and optional materials are there if you want to go further.

Milestones

Between classes, there is a short milestone that applies that week's material to your own work. There are five milestones, worth 10 points each, and each is due before the start of the next class. The instructions and the submission form for each one are on its assignment page.

Final Project

The course ends with one shared project, done in groups. Every group receives the same data and answers a question of its own choosing for a stated audience. The topic, the data, and a detailed brief will be released in class. After the deadline, each student reviews at least three other groups' projects with the course rubric. The project is worth 25 points, and its grade combines the instructor and TA (40%), peer reviews (40%), and an automated AI review (20%), all scored on one rubric.

Optional Tasks

Four optional tasks let you earn additional points, up to 15 in total. They are done individually.

  • Confidently Wrong (10 points): a finance question on which a frontier model gives a wrong answer with full confidence, with the correct answer and why the model went wrong.
  • Right for the Wrong Reason (10 points): a finance question on which a model reaches the correct answer through invalid reasoning, with the step that fails.
  • In the Wild (10 points): a confirmed case from the past 12 months in which a large financial firm published work containing an AI-generated error.
  • Share Your Setup (5 points): a five-minute live demonstration in class of the AI setup you have built for yourself.

Overview

There are five short milestones between classes and one final group project. Each milestone applies that week's material to your own work, and each is due before the start of the next class. Full instructions and the submission form for each one are on its assignment page.

Groups

Milestone #3 and the final project are done in groups of up to five, and the same group does both. You form your own group and join one of the Project Groups on Canvas before Milestone #3 is due. All other milestones are individual.

Final Project

Every group receives the same data pack and builds its site in VS Code with Claude Code, hosted on GitHub Pages. The topic, the data pack, and a detailed brief will be released in class. Each student also reviews at least three other groups' sites with the course rubric. The grade combines the instructor and TA (40%), peer reviews (40%), and an automated AI review (20%), all scored on one rubric.

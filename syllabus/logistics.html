Canvas and This Website

We will use two sites. This website holds the syllabus, the readings, the course materials for each week, and the instructions for every assignment. Each assignment page says where and how to submit it. Canvas (CourseWorks) holds your grades and the project groups, and announcements and messages from the teaching team go through Canvas, so please check your notifications regularly.

Class Meetings

We meet once a week for a single three-hour session, which mixes lecture, discussion, and in-class exercises. Each class begins with a short quiz on that week's readings, done on paper without devices. As a baseline, laptops stay closed unless I ask you to open them. This course follows the MBA attendance guidelines.

Individual and Group Work

Most of the work in this course is individual. Some projects are done in groups; each assignment page says which. Groups are formed on Canvas.

Grading

Your grade is based on the total number of points you earn, subject to the CBS elective grading curve, which applies to the overall score and not to individual items. Details on each item are in Deliverables.

Use of AI

AI use in this course is not only permitted but required. For the milestones and the final project, use AI as much as you can, and add a brief note on which tools you used and how their output entered your work. Honest disclosure never lowers your grade. The in-class quizzes are completed entirely without AI.

Honor Code and Accommodations

All work in this course is subject to the CBS Honor Code. Course materials are for your use in this course only; do not upload them to external sites or share them outside the class without my permission. Students with documented disabilities may receive reasonable accommodations through Columbia's Office of Disability Services.
